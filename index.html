Research - In Progress TWSE / MICROSTRUCTURE

Pre-Market Limit Order Book Modeling

Tests whether the TWSE pre-open call auction book (08:30–09:00, no execution) carries information about the opening price. Because the opening price itself cannot be traded against, the forecast is expressed via index futures, which open at 08:45—15 minutes before the cash market. Independent continuation of research begun during an internship at SQM Technologies.

Dataset: 5-level order book snapshots (5-second intervals) for 50 TWSE constituents over 13 trading days (~650 day-stock samples).
Status: Validated data pipeline and evaluation framework. CNN-LSTM and MLP iterations complete; DeepLOB adaptation (Zhang et al., IEEE TSP 2019) in progress.
